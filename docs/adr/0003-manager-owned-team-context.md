# Manager-owned team context

Team notes belong to a manager/person and are inherited along the current formal
reporting chain, not informal membership or an independently modeled permanent
team. Reporting transitions preserve the former chain and locally known note
versions in historical context, while current inheritance follows the new chain.
This avoids guessing stable team identities or transferring notes to replacement
leaders, at the cost of explicitly flagging potentially stale manager-owned
context for review after role or reporting changes.
