## Scoped disposable-worker housekeeping

V4 changes **no timing value or evidence rule**. It permits a visible warning
only for a real `UnauthorizedAccessException`/AccessDenied refusal deleting
the run-owned x64 compiled `e_sqlite3.dll` in an expected project's isolated
`build\bin\<project>\release\runtimes\win-x64\native` output, after complete
required verification and within the original whole-execution budget.
The actual cleanup outcome stays **failed**, with exact error, HRESULT, path,
phase and remaining-build metadata. The holder is unknown; this is policy,
not a causal repair or a claim that deletion succeeded.

The runner produces a fresh nonce-bound raw proof and exits without accepting
verification before the standard `actions/attest@v4` signing handoff. Completion
verifies that proof within the original budget. A supervised process receipt
preserves execution errors even if terminal result writing preceded a later
exception. `gh attestation verify` checks the real signature and witnessed time;
positive `signature.certificate.runnerEnvironment=github-hosted`, repository
identifiers and authenticated run/attempt/workflow context are mandatory.
Signer/controller source B and checked-out candidate A are verified separately,
including immutable reusable-workflow digests and each source's effective
policy. Predicate hosting claims, environment flags, copied IDs and artifact
versions are not authority. No bearer/request token or private signing key is
retained. Only measurement jobs and their necessary callers receive signing
permissions; workers cannot write baseline history.

Signing, waiting and verification count inside the unchanged budget. Missing,
invalid, stale or mismatched proof, failed work, known running owned commands,
unknown source/runtime scope, local/shared/self-hosted execution, unexpected
targets/error types and budget exhaustion remain failures. Required raw
21-sample percentiles, matrices, phases, TRX, actual native/runtime identities
and logical handoffs are still validated normally. Compact history retains
signed inputs and failed housekeeping independently of workload validation.

Artifact upload and union copies exclude the allocated compiled build subtree,
including its DLL and generated build metadata, while preserving raw metrics,
TRX, phase logs, lifecycle/Join snapshots, PNGs and cleanup/provenance diagnostics.
Only normal disposable-VM teardown is relied on: no deletion retry, accepting
time extension, shared-server shutdown, AV change or holder-PID kill.
Housekeeping warnings alone never request samples4/5 or a clean retry.
Otherwise-qualified main cohorts retain ordinary approved eligibility rules;
manual, diagnostic, pilot, fork and local observations remain ineligible.

V1/v2/v3 sources, previews and stored verdicts retain their original semantics,
including cohorts45775685351 and45787987253. #154 ended inconclusively; v4 does
not identify their holder or turn those failures into successful verification.

Version2 retains the **empirical-envelope rule**
whose numerical bands are derived from a pinned eligible comparable cohort.
The new practical-significance floors supplement that evidence rule; they do
not claim calibrated statistical confidence.

For each selected metric retain the independent per-execution values, count,
minimum L, maximum U, median M and full observed range R=U-L. A user's21 raw
latency samples still produce that execution's original nearest-rank p95;
three p95 values from three workers are not63 independent executions.

900 seconds is a **bounded safety limit**, not a predicted healthy p95:
it reuses the already authorized observation horizon, which captured683.0593558s
with216.9406442s remaining. The diagnostic alone cannot establish its future
success rate. No other workload receives a blanket extension. Watchdog reserve
is reporting/termination/upload allowance, never additional accepting time.

execution:900s safety stays separate. Activation must preserve measurement/setup/
cleanup cost rather than subtract it. The historical diagnostic still used17

For a positive observed reference range:

- Green comparison: complete measured inputs; candidate maximum<=U and
  candidate range<=R, goals met and remaining applicable headroom>R.
- Yellow: maximum>U, range>R, missed existing goal, or headroom<=R. One observed
  range of headroom means an already observed fluctuation could exhaust it.
  Yellow alone never blocks merge/release.
- Serious-relative red: **five** complete eligible comparable candidate
  executions, every value>U+R, candidate range<=R, and median increase>R.
  Also require **both** median increase>=20% and absolute increase>=100ms for
  selected product latency, or **both**>=20% and>=30s for whole duration.
  All practical boundaries are inclusive; the existing beyond-envelope `>`
  conditions remain strict. One slow worker or only one practical floor cannot
  establish serious red. This is an approved deterministic evidence/practical
  rule, not an experimentally calibrated probability. Phase/editor/navigation
  observations remain nonblocking.
- A zero reference range cannot establish a calibrated noise floor: comparative
  health is inconclusive, not a threshold where any epsilon is a serious
  regression. Hard requirements and absolute goals still apply.
- Missing/zero reference median cannot establish percentage significance.
  Keep the percentage unavailable and relative comparison inconclusive while
  evaluating known absolute requirements. A1500ms rich-note p95 satisfies v2's
  ceiling; any value>1500ms violates it immediately, without a reference or five
  runs. A1000ms goal miss within1500ms remains yellow unless the separate
  evidence **and both practical floors** establish serious-relative red.
- Any individual hard violation remains red/actionable under **current** policy,
  even if the cohort median is below its limit. Missing/censored metrics and
  interrupted required work cannot supply green comparison or acceptance.

## Eligibility, comparison series and automatic promotion

The following approved policy is implemented by the trusted automatic collector
under explicit `ci-timing-v2`. Preview replay remains read-only, with promotion
disabled. Production reference establishment and complete hosted evidence remain
part of the joint delivery/#133 verification, not claims made by local fixtures.

Start with exactly three independent fresh complete executions, each owning
its required non-UI, actual prefix snapshot, fresh Join/tail and strict union.
All must pass the policy that was actually enforced, share comparison keys and
retain exact candidate/run/attempt/job/runner/invocation provenance. No failed,
skipped, cancelled, missing, unrequested or reused execution can be removed or
replaced to obtain three greens. Pilot/manual/fork/local/diagnostic observations
are never canonical inputs.

With a compatible reference, otherwise complete yellow/out-of-envelope or wider
range evidence proposes two additional fresh executions, numbered4 and5. Stop
at five. Persistent wider range or low headroom is inconclusive and retains
the prior reference. A complete consistent five-execution yellow cohort may
promote after comparison if it is neither a hard failure nor serious-relative
red; warning history survives. No failure authorizes this extension.

For the first eligible main cohort of a new series, compare unavailable before
bootstrap; never claim comparative green. Retain its actual median/range as an
initial empirical envelope. If its measured range spans remaining safety/hard
headroom, extend otherwise eligible evidence to five; unresolved ambiguity
retains no reference. Fixed healthy/noise bands for unsupported workloads remain
pending, not automatic requirements that release wait for collection.

Pin the available reference and history tip **before** evaluation. Exclude
the candidate itself; use its recorded eligible predecessor if already active.
Preserve comparison and prior revision/age before any replacement. Use Git
main ancestry/order, not completion time, for promotion: an older cohort finishing
late cannot replace a newer eligible one. Unknown ancestry or publication
failure is explicit and retains the previous reference. The preview's eligible
prior observation is expressly not a canonical active-reference pointer.

Canonical bootstrap/plan/three-sample decision/reference events are immutable
records on the existing data branch. Initial bootstrap selects verified latest
main once under explicit trusted planning, then remains pinned as main advances.
The shared publisher first appends the comparison, then eligible promotion;
conflict reconciliation keeps that comparison and every attempt unchanged.
The effective pointer is the newest eligible event in actual main first-parent
order, not a mutable timestamp-based file. Missing source/configuration remains
explicit and cannot authorize a promotion.

Keys include workload/selector, ordered scenarios/phases/occurrences, full rich
population/size/sample matrices, metric unit/boundary/percentile/effective policy,
fixture/harness/test identity, resolved runtime/SDK/native binding, OS/architecture,
image/version/processors, actual display/DPI and setup/cache/controlled-clock/
observation scope. Missing relevant identity means incompatible/inconclusive.
Rich prefix and tail comparison requires the actual versioned display state,

resolved native version/source/hash matching the independently validated
prefix handoff and tail receipt binding. Nonblank placeholders or a different

does not invalidate an otherwise intact workload: retain its raw acceptance,

requesting replacement samples. Cached series hashes or eligibility fields
cannot substitute for those actual configurations at canonical evaluation.
Standalone prefix/tail observations retain that validated native binding and
apply the same tuple matcher during live and historical reference selection.
Legacy observation verdicts and hashes remain intact; missing retained actual
binding is comparison-unavailable, never automatic compatibility.
Retained hashes use their original generic configuration/hash contract; newer

Application commit/binary hashes remain provenance across revisions, not equality

Changed compatible workload/environment definitions may bootstrap a new eligible
series automatically under the approved algorithm, never auto-change hard
